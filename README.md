# Godot Cave
A simple platformer game.
<img width="998" height="474" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a45dc810-8277-4d90-bab0-f76adb2d548b" />
You can play **Godot Cave** at: https://marygoat.itch.io/tempo-surge
To start, just press run!

### Controls

- A D / Left Right to move
- Space / W / Up to jump

### Features 

A simple tile based game where you must reach the exit of the cave, where you play as the Godot Icon!

Made in Godot and was made for the SnowGlobe Hackclub program, with the 2D platform guide!

This was made to be a simple 2D platform game just to learn how to make one, but I am thinking of coming back to this to update it and make it more unique and personal.

Hope you have fun playing **Godot Cave!**
